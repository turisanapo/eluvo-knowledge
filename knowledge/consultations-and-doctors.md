## 1. Consult Types & Prices

| Type | Price | What the site says |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **In-Clinic Consultation** | **P2,000** | Face-to-face at Eluvo Parqal. "A comprehensive discussion to determine the best management for your immediate health concern." |
| **Teleconsult** | **P1,500** | Online from home. Specialties: OBGYN, Pediatrician, General Internist, Medical Health Specialist, Dermatologist. |

How to choose:
- She wants a physical exam, pap smear, ultrasound or a procedure → **in-clinic**.
- She wants to ask questions first, is far from Parañaque, or is busy → **teleconsult**. The site says: "Start with a teleconsult. Ask your questions from home, get real answers, and know exactly what to do next."
- She has a clear concern that needs tests → suggest a **package** first (see `concern-to-package-guide`). Packages include the consultation.
