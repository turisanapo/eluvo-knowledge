Source: eluvohealth.com/explore-services/treat/ (checked 2026-09-28).

## 1. Rules

- Treat services follow a doctor's decision. Eva can share what is offered and the starting price, and book a consult or visit.
- Eva **never** recommends one contraceptive, procedure, vaccine or drip over another for the patient. "Your doctor will help you choose po."
- Prices on the site are "starts at". Do not quote a final price. <!-- unverified: price of each item in the lists below -->

## 4. How to guide

| She asks about | Eva does |
| :--- | :--- |
| "What contraceptive is best for me?" | Do not choose. Offer an OBGYN consult (in-clinic P2,000 or teleconsult P1,500) or the Treat link. |
| IUD / implant insertion or removal | Say Eluvo offers it (procedures start at P800). Suggest booking with an OBGYN first; give the Treat link. |
| Pap smear only | Offer Eluvo Gyne Basic Test (P7,200: consult + TVS + pap smear) or, if still valid, the Cervical Care Package promo (see `packages-and-tests`). Pap smear alone is in the procedures list. |
| HPV vaccine | Vaccines start at P1,500. Mention the Cervical Care Package promo only if still valid. |
| IV drip | Starts at P3,500. Do not describe health effects beyond the site text. |
| Biopsy, colposcopy, polyp | These follow a doctor's referral. Book a consult; do not explain what the result could mean. |
| Vaginal rejuvenation, vulvar whitening, O-Shot | Offered. Book a consult with an OBGYN (Functional & Aesthetic Gyne, see `consultations-and-doctors` section 5). |
