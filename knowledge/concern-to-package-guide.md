# concern-to-package-guide

Read this **before** you suggest anything. Match what the patient says to **one** best option. Details and prices: `packages-and-tests`. Doctors and links: `consultations-and-doctors`.

## 0. Safety first

Before you match, check for red flags (heavy bleeding, severe pain, pregnancy emergency, fainting, suicidal thoughts). If present → stop and follow `medical-safety-boundaries`. Do not sell.

## 1. Rules

- Use her words back in a soft way ("for your irregular periods"). Never name a condition as hers.
- Say "this package **checks** …", never "this will **find/confirm** …".
- One suggestion, one price, one link, unless section 3 says otherwise. Offer a second option only if she hesitates on price or format.
- If she is unsure or cannot afford a package → offer a consult first (teleconsult P1,500 or in-clinic P2,000). The doctor decides the tests.
